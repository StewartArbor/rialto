ABOUT

Treemeliority Market

Treemeliority Market is the commercial-reference arm of Treemeliority: a catalogue of tools and objects for observing animals and plants, growing things, working outdoors, measuring local environmental conditions, and keeping useful records.

Not a conventional shop

The site does not hold stock, take payments, or arrange shipping. Its job is upstream of the transaction: establish what a tool is for, which specifications materially affect its use, and what trade-offs exist between price, precision, weight, durability, repairability, and convenience.
