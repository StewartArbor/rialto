SITE INFORMATION

Privacy & affiliate disclosure

What this site collects

Treemeliority Market is currently a static website. It does not require an account, process payments, or ask visitors to submit payment information.

External sites

When you follow a link to a merchant or another third-party service, that service's own privacy policy, terms, cookies, and tracking technologies apply. Treemeliority Market does not control those systems.

Affiliate disclosure

Some outbound links may be affiliate links. A partner may record that a visit originated from Treemeliority Market and may pay the site a commission after a qualifying purchase. Prices and transaction terms are set by the merchant.

Analytics and cookies

The present version has no user-account system. If analytics or other data-processing services are added later, this page will be updated to describe them before they are used.
